# Movocco

## Overview

Movocco is a transportation and logistics platform designed to help tourists navigate Morocco with confidence.

The goal is simple:

**Help travelers move around Morocco like locals while avoiding confusion, overcharging, and transportation uncertainty.**

Movocco provides practical transportation information, route planning, transport hub locations, vehicle identification guides, and community feedback to make traveling easier and safer.

---

## Problem

Many tourists visiting Morocco face transportation challenges:

* Not knowing how much a trip should cost
* Difficulty finding Grand Taxi stations
* Confusion between different vehicle types
* Uncertainty about the best route to a destination
* Limited access to reliable local transportation information

Movocco aims to solve these problems through accurate, easy-to-access transportation guidance.

---

## Solution

Movocco serves as a digital transportation guide for travelers.

Users can:

* Check transportation prices between destinations
* Locate Grand Taxi stations and transport hubs
* Identify vehicle types using visual guides
* Plan routes across multiple destinations
* Read reviews and travel feedback
* Access transportation recommendations and tips

---

## Core Features

### Transport Price Calculator

Provides estimated transportation costs between destinations.

Supported transport methods:

* Grand Taxi
* Bus
* InDrive

Users can compare options and better understand expected travel expenses.

---

### Route Planner

Helps travelers discover the best way to reach their destination.

The planner provides:

* Recommended route
* Transportation sequence
* Estimated cost
* Estimated travel time

Example:

Agadir Airport → Agadir Station → Tamri → Imsouane

---

### Taxi Hub Locator

Interactive map displaying:

* Grand Taxi stations
* Transport hubs
* Major departure points

Users can quickly locate where to catch transportation to their desired destination.

---

### Vehicle Guide

Visual guide to common transportation methods in Morocco.

Includes:

* Grand Taxis
* Petit Taxis
* CTM Buses
* Supratours Buses

Each guide contains useful information, photos, capacity details, and travel tips.

---

### Reviews & Feedback

Community-driven section where travelers can:

* Share experiences
* Leave reviews
* Suggest updates
* Report inaccurate information

This helps maintain data quality and trust.

---

## Target Audience

Movocco is designed for:

* International tourists
* Backpackers
* Digital nomads
* Solo travelers
* First-time visitors to Morocco

---

## Vision

To become the most trusted transportation and travel logistics platform for visitors exploring Morocco.

Movocco aims to bridge the gap between local transportation knowledge and tourist accessibility, making travel across Morocco simpler, safer, and more transparent.
